Scope and Purpose

MDSynapse.org publishes analysis of medical artificial intelligence for clinicians, researchers, and others working at the intersection of medicine and computation.

The objective is accurate characterization of what these methods have been shown to do, under what conditions, and with what confidence. Claims are traced to their primary sources, quantitative results are reported as published, and limitations are stated explicitly rather than deferred.

The material is intended for readers who evaluate these systems in practice: clinicians deciding whether to rely on a tool, researchers designing validation studies, and institutions making procurement decisions.
